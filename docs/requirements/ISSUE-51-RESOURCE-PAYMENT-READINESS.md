# Issues #41 / #43 / #51 — Resource-backed customer confirmation and payment

## Development scope

- The generic customer booking endpoint must not commit a confirmed reservation solely from a published ServiceItem and price snapshot. The transaction now checks matching approved typed allocations before consuming the quote; failure rolls back the booking and leaves the quote unused. No confirmed customer/manager notification is sent for rejection.
- The generic customer modal now posts to the existing enquiry endpoint, with request-only wording. Its price is an indicative server estimate, not final availability, payable checkout or approved cancellation terms. Existing six specialized enquiry forms and dedicated bike Booking checkout remain separate.
- Payment initiation and verification require an active matching branch/item, complete increasing start/end schedule, and confirmed typed allocation(s) for exactly the same booking, branch, item, interval and units. Released/held/draft, wrong type/window/branch/quantity and unavailable capacity fail. This is rechecked on the locked booking immediately before receipt/accounting updates.
- Taxi additionally requires both vehicle and driver allocations and a matching dispatch, currently eligible documented cab/driver and passenger capacity. Other service patterns currently support one typed resource with allocation quantity matching booking quantity; split allocations and service-specific charter/room/group rules require their own reviewed orchestration. This guard is not a declaration that five service operational modules are complete.
- New quote snapshots store versioned reservation context in existing breakdown JSON: end time (including explicit null), branch and price unit. Booking validation binds these in addition to owner, item, service type, start, quantity, expiry and single use. Online payment also rechecks accepted quote identity/quantity/start/end/context/total against the current booking. Missing legacy context fails closed; historical rows are not rewritten or given fabricated approval.
- No schema migration, invented tariff/cancellation policy, automatic resource selection or automatic dispatch has been added. Generic previews do not implement verified route distance, room-night pricing or full service-specific terms; those remain approval/workflow dependencies.

## Tests and release gates

Automated cases cover unallocated confirmed-status payment attempts across all six non-bike services; positive payments on synthetic genuinely allocated capacity; tampering and duplicate receipts; released/mismatched resources; changed accepted quote interval; allocation release after mocked provider verification and before locked accounting; generic booking rollback/no notification; changed quote end/null/branch/unit and missing legacy context. Quote/payment cases are also explicitly invoked in disposable MySQL CI. Only executed current-head CI results count as evidence.

Before deployment: audit existing online/offline reservations and missing allocation/quote context privately, reconcile paid/captured provider records, do not backfill fabricated resources or historical quotes, and obtain owner-reviewed staging acceptance. A provider payment captured before a failed late eligibility check needs independent finance reconciliation (#103/#106); an error response does not prove no external funds moved. No automatic refund is authorized here.

Remaining: atomic service-specific approval/allocation/confirmation orchestration (including legacy/admin creation/transition paths), approved duration-aware tariffs and complete immutable terms/resource selection in payable quotes, provider inbox/reconciliation and genuine cross-worker payment/cancellation tests, all-service staff/browser acceptance and owner sign-off. Issues remain open. No real customer sends, gateway charge, refund or deployed DB change is performed by this development slice.
