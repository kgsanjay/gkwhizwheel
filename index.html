Frequently Asked Questions

Essential answers to help you explore your next booking.

What documents are required to rent a bike?

A valid driving licence and identity verification may be required. Confirm the latest requirements with the branch before your trip.

How much is the security deposit and when is it refunded?

Deposits vary by vehicle and booking terms. Check the rental details or contact the team for an accurate amount and refund policy.

What is your cancellation and booking rescheduling policy?

Cancellation and rescheduling rules depend on the service and selected package. Review the policy on the original website before booking.

What happens if the bike breaks down or gets a flat tyre?

Contact the rental branch immediately and follow the roadside support instructions supplied with your rental.
